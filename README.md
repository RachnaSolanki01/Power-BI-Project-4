Employee Workforce & HR Analytics Dashboard

📊 Project Overview

This Power BI project is an Employee Workforce & HR Analytics Dashboard designed to provide insights into employee headcount, attrition, salary, workforce structure, career levels, employee status, and hiring trends.

The dashboard is divided into three interactive pages to make HR information easy to understand and analyze.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze total, active, and terminated employee headcount

Monitor employee attrition rate

Identify high-performing employees

Analyze average salary across departments

Understand workforce distribution by employee type

Analyze headcount by department, gender, and employee status

Track new-hire trends over time

Analyze workforce distribution by career level

Compare departmental salary rankings

Identify employees below expectations

📑 Dashboard Pages

1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level workforce summary using KPI cards:

Total Headcount

Terminated Employees

Active Headcount

Attrition Rate

High Performers

Average Salary

High Performer %

2. Workforce & Department Analysis

Focuses on workforce composition and department-level analysis:

Average Salary & Salary Rank by Department

Headcount by Employee Type

Headcount & New Hires by Year

Headcount by Department

Year-to-Date New Hires by Month

Year Slicer for interactive filtering

3. Career & Hiring Analysis

Provides deeper workforce and career-related insights:

Employees Below Expectations by Department

Department Salary & Rank Summary

Headcount by Employee Status

Headcount by Gender

Workforce by Career Level Band

🔍 Dashboard Interactivity

Users can interact with the dashboard using slicers and visuals to explore workforce information across different years, departments, employee types, career levels, and other dimensions.

💡 Business Insights

The dashboard helps HR and management:

Monitor workforce size and employee turnover

Understand department-level workforce distribution

Review salary patterns across departments

Track hiring activity and trends

Analyze employee diversity

Understand career-level distribution

Identify high-performing and below-expectation workforce segments

📌 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how Power BI, Power Query, DAX, data modeling, and interactive visualization can be used to transform employee data into an easy-to-understand HR analytics dashboard.
